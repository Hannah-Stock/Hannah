Kids Conditioning

Kids Conditioning is a structured training program designed to improve strength, speed, coordination, and overall athletic ability. Each session focuses on proper movement, confidence, and building a strong foundation for sports and everyday activity in a fun, supportive environment.

What do the athletes get...

  • Program runs 6 weeks at a time
  • 1 private session a week for 30 minutes at South Park Fairgrounds
  • Workouts tailored towards their goals
  • Extra workouts given to do on own

Time Slots

  • 4:10-4:40pm
  • 4:50-5:20pm
  • 5:30-6:00pm
  • 6:10-6:40pm
  • 6:50-7:20pm
Program begins May 13th and runs in 6-week sessions. Athletes may join at any time, but early registration is recommended to secure your preferred time slot. Additional time slots will open as current sessions are completed.
